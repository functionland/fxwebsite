Functionland Inc, Fula Governance Association and the Fula network

Functionland Inc

Functionland Inc was a corporation organized under the laws of Canada. It developed FxBlox hardware and the FxBlox and FxFiles apps, and it received grants from Fula Governance Association to work on the development, marketing and maintenance of the Fula network. With development completed and the major parts of the network in place and running, there is no further need for Functionland Inc. It was dissolved on September 9, 2026. Its domains and servers were transferred to Fula Governance Association.

Fula Governance Association

Fula Governance Association was an association organized under the laws of Switzerland. With development completed and the FULA token's distribution and rewards managed by smart contracts, there is no further need for the association. It was dissolved with effect from November 3, 2026.

Fula today

Fula is now carried forward by its community, including developers who volunteer to contribute to the future of decentralized data and AI. The network runs on individually owned nodes and deployed smart contracts.

Apart from the tokens granted for the support described below, the FULA token's distribution, staking rewards and reserves are managed by smart contracts. Users claim directly from each contract through the portal, without an admin sending them tokens.

Separately, under a grant agreement with Fula Governance Association, a development team received ether and FULA tokens to provide support for the Fula community and the open-source applications for at least 18 months from September 16, 2026. This website, its domains and the apps have been transferred from Fula Governance Association to that team.
