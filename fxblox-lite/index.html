FxBlox Lite

  • Dual Access: Engage with FxBlox Lite on Linux Desktop or from the FxBlox web app — Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS or Linux), or on an Android phone.
  • Efficiency Meets Speed: Runs 3x faster than Raspberry Pi 4 while consuming 1/10 power of a modern laptop.
  • Easy Modularity: 3 USB-C slots for effortless customization; no tools required.
  • Feature-Packed: Dual 4K, onboard storage, RGB LEDs, USB-C Power Delivery, and wireless connectivity.
  • Versatile Uses: Ideal for Desktops, Streaming, Smart Home, Blockchain Nodes. Dive into open-source and say goodbye to Cloud price hikes.

Plug and Play Desktop Computer

Specifications

Processor
RK3588 octa-core (4x Cortex-A76 + 4x Cortex-A55)
RAM Options
8 GB / 32 GB LPDDR4X
Storage Options
1 TB / 2 TB NVMe SSD
Display Output
Dual 4K (HDMI + USB-C)
USB Ports
3x USB-C (modular expansion slots)
Connectivity
Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.2, Gigabit Ethernet
Power
USB-C Power Delivery

Linux (open-source) + FxBlox web app (Chrome or Edge)
Indicators
RGB LED status lights
Design
Tool-free modular assembly
