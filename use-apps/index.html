FxFiles

Experience the Power of Blockchain-Attached Storage — an upgrade to NAS. Synchronize any file with the Fula DePIN network.

  • Full Privacy — all data is encrypted on your device before leaving it
  • Audio Playlist Manager — advanced playlists with visualizer and background playback
  • Face Detection — automatically group photos by the people in them
  • File Tags & Categorization — organize files with custom tags and smart categories
  • AI Website Generator — create a website from your files with one click and share with anyone, no domain or hosting needed
  • Encrypted Cloud Backup — files are encrypted and replicated across the Fula network
  • Local-First for Blox Owners — a full copy of your files lives on your own Blox, accessible even if the internet is down or censored
  • Secure Sharing — create a share link for any file with full privacy, just like a normal cloud link

How can I use the applications?

1

Install

Get the application from your preferred method, either Appstore on iOS, Google Play on Android or other means.

2

Login

Currently login is with Google and Apple, but it does not mean your data is sent to their servers. We only use them to generate your private keys.

3

Get Storage

Currently you need to get Fula tokens to cover the cost of using and storing data on Fula network, which is up to 2 times cheaper than central cloud.

Discover the Advantages!

Increased Privacy

Your data remains entirely yours, eliminating central vulnerabilities and ensuring optimal security.

Data is encrypted on-device and split into pieces duplicated across the blockchain network.

Affordable Storage

Storage is significantly cheaper due to low-electricity device operation, eliminating expensive server infrastructure.

Low-power nodes keep operational costs minimal, passing the savings directly to users.
